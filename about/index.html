About Free Image to Text

Free Image to Text is a privacy-first OCR web app for Gentil M. (gentil.quevvy.com), based in Kigali, Rwanda. It builds on the MIT-licensed ocr-tool project and Tesseract.js.

Images never leave your device. The site is free to use and supported by ethical Adsterra placements when enabled.
