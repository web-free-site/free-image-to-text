How to copy text from a photo

When you have a picture of a page, a whiteboard, or a receipt, you should not have to retype it by hand. Optical character recognition (OCR) can turn that picture into text you can paste. Free Image to Text runs the OCR in your browser.

Steps

  1. Use a sharp, well-lit photo. Avoid blur, heavy shadows, and a skewed page when you can. A straight, high-resolution picture usually gives cleaner text.
  2. Open the OCR tool and add the image. Go to Free Image to Text and choose the photo from your device. The image is processed locally.
  3. Choose the language. Select English, French, or Swahili to match the text in the picture. The wrong language model will increase errors.
  4. Copy and proofread the result. Copy the text, then compare it with the photo. Names, numbers, and accented characters are the first things to check.

What OCR gets wrong

Handwriting, stylized fonts, stamps, and low-resolution screenshots often produce mistakes. Treat the output as a draft, not as a certified transcript.

Privacy

Because recognition runs in the browser, you do not need to upload the photo to this site. You are still responsible for how you store or share the copied text.

Try it on Free Image to Text, or use the dedicated JPG to text page.
